# Marketplace campaign assets

Created with the built-in image generation tool for the supplied marketplace reference.
These are representative campaign illustrations, not photographs of verified merchants.
Storefront previews carry an illustrative demo label. No business records or metrics were added.

- `public/market-hero-v2.png`: wide Indian kirana scene; smiling shopkeeper handing a kraft grocery bag to a green-jacket delivery rider; warm natural daylight; blank ivory left third for live HTML copy; no text, logo or watermark.
- `public/market-categories-v2.png`: equal 5-column, 2-row photographic contact sheet. Top row: vegetables, fruits, milk/bread/eggs, burger/fries, Indian meal. Bottom row: sweets/bakery, clothing/shoes, meat/seafood, electronics, personal/household care. Pastel backgrounds; no text or watermark.
- `public/market-storefronts-v2.png`: equal 5-column single-row contact sheet of representative Indian kirana, produce stall, dairy, bakery and tea merchant exteriors. Warm daylight; no readable signs, logos, ratings or UI.

The original SVG brand mark and working product imagery remain in use. Actual merchant cover images take precedence over representative demo previews.
